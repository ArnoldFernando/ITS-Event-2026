About the Event

Where Technology Meets Teamwork

CICS Battle of Legends is a full-day Information Technology Skills Festival presented by the Information Technology Society of the College of Information and Computing Sciences. Students and faculty unite across five teams to celebrate programming, digital creativity, and technical problem-solving — powered by an esports atmosphere, not an esports tournament.
